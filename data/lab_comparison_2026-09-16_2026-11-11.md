## Interpretation and follow-up questions

- Lower fasting glucose/insulin and HOMA-IR are descriptive and compatible with metabolic improvement, but are not a validated within-subject causal estimate.
- Discuss creatinine rise with a clinician, given exercise, muscularity, creatine and potential dehydration; consider urine albumin/creatinine and cystatin C as clinically appropriate.
- Discuss lower serum iron and transferrin, near-upper-range ferritin and hemoglobin change; serum iron alone does not diagnose iron deficiency; consider transferrin saturation, CBC and inflammatory context.
- Persistent vitamin D <20 ng/mL requires clinician review of supplementation, adherence and possible causes.
- Hormones fluctuate, and the laboratory values neither establish medication effects nor justify hormone treatment.
- No direct evidence of absence or presence of systemic inflammation or tendon structural healing is provided by these tests. A CBC alone is not a suitable test for these claims.
- ALT/AST, HbA1c and urinary albumin were not included in the 18-page November panel; **missing is not normal**. Prior reported gastrointestinal symptoms warrant clinical review if persistent or severe.
