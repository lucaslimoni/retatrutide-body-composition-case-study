# References and verification notes

These publications provide **background, not proof of the cause of observed case changes**.

1. CARE Case Report Guidelines. **CARE Checklist** (2013). https://www.care-statement.org/checklist
2. Jastreboff AM, Kaplan LM, Frías JP, et al. **Triple–Hormone-Receptor Agonist Retatrutide for Obesity — A Phase 2 Trial.** *New England Journal of Medicine* 2023;389:514–526. DOI: [10.1056/NEJMoa2301972](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2301972). PMID: [37366315](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37366315/). This trial studied a characterized trial drug under controlled conditions; results should **not be equated with an unverified commercially sourced product**.
3. U.S. National Library of Medicine / ClinicalTrials.gov. **NCT04881760**, a phase 2 study of retatrutide (LY3437943). https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04881760

**Evidence hierarchy:** this public N=1 diary was not randomized or blinded and has no matched untreated control group; its descriptive changes cannot confirm efficacy, safety or causality.
